ContextMover

Summary from package

Never lose coding context again. One layer. Any agent. Zero friction.

Description
ContextMover is the missing layer between AI platforms. It captures your conversations from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, and Perplexity — then lets you migrate the full context to any other platform in one click. No copying. No pasting. No starting over.

─────────────────────────────────────
WHY CONTEXTMOVER EXISTS
─────────────────────────────────────

Every AI platform has different strengths. Claude is exceptional at reasoning and long documents. ChatGPT has the largest plugin ecosystem. Gemini integrates with Google Workspace. Grok has real-time web access. DeepSeek excels at code. Perplexity specializes in research.

Power users don't live on one platform. They switch based on the task. But every switch means losing context — re-explaining your project, your constraints, your history. That friction kills productivity.

ContextMover eliminates that friction entirely.

─────────────────────────────────────
HOW IT WORKS
─────────────────────────────────────

ContextMover works by intercepting the API responses that AI platforms send to your own browser — the same responses that render on your screen. It captures those responses locally, stores them in your browser's IndexedDB, and makes them available for migration.

When you migrate to a new platform, ContextMover formats the captured context optimally for the destination model:
- Claude receives context in structured XML format
- ChatGPT receives context in Markdown
- Gemini and others receive clean plain text

This format-aware migration means the receiving model understands the context as naturally as possible — not as a raw dump, but as a structured conversation it can immediately build on.

─────────────────────────────────────
THE ATTENTION ENGINE
─────────────────────────────────────

Long AI conversations can contain hundreds of messages. Migrating all of them verbatim would exceed context window limits and overwhelm the receiving model with irrelevant detail.

ContextMover includes a local Attention Engine — a transformer-based semantic indexing system that runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. It analyzes your conversation, identifies the most relevant messages for your current task, and compresses the context intelligently before migration.

The Attention Engine runs in an offscreen document so it never affects your browser's main thread performance. On low-end hardware it yields between processing batches to keep your browser responsive.

No conversation data is sent to ContextMover servers for this analysis. The ML model runs locally on your device.

─────────────────────────────────────
ZERO-KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE
─────────────────────────────────────

ContextMover is built on a zero-knowledge principle: your conversations are yours.

- All session capture happens locally in your browser
- The Attention Engine runs locally via WebAssembly
- Captured sessions are stored in your browser's local IndexedDB
- When you migrate, the context is sent directly from your browser to the destination AI platform — it does not pass through ContextMover servers
- ContextMover servers only handle: account authentication, usage counters, and subscription status

We cannot read your conversations. We do not want to. The architecture makes it technically impossible even if we did.

─────────────────────────────────────
FEATURES
─────────────────────────────────────

ONE-CLICK MIGRATION
Switch AI platforms without losing your work. Select a captured session in the side panel, choose the destination platform, and click Migrate. ContextMover opens the destination platform and injects the context automatically.

AUTOMATIC SESSION CAPTURE
Every conversation on every supported platform is captured automatically as you chat. No manual steps. Open the side panel to see your full session library organized by platform and date.

SMART CONTEXT COMPRESSION
The Attention Engine compresses long conversations to fit destination context windows, preserving the most relevant information for your current task using semantic similarity scoring.

FORMAT-AWARE MIGRATION
Context is formatted specifically for each destination model. Claude gets XML. ChatGPT gets Markdown. This maximizes comprehension quality at the destination.

SESSION LIBRARY
Browse, search, and revisit all your AI conversations across all platforms in one unified library. Sessions are stored locally and persist across browser restarts.

EXPORT SESSIONS
Export any captured session as XML, Markdown, or plain text. Use them in your own tools, feed them into scripts, archive them, or share them with teammates.

PRIORITY MIGRATION QUEUE
User-triggered migrations run immediately on a priority lane. Background sync operations pause and resume automatically, ensuring migrations are always fast regardless of background activity.

PARTIAL SYNC FOR NEW ACCOUNTS
When you sign in on a new device, ContextMover syncs your 3 most recent sessions immediately so you can work right away, then syncs older sessions in the background without blocking the UI.

USAGE TRANSPARENCY
Free tier users can see exactly how many migrations remain this month directly in the extension badge. Warnings appear at 3 remaining. Hard limits are enforced server-side with row-level locking — no silent overages.

─────────────────────────────────────
PRIVACY & SECURITY
─────────────────────────────────────

ContextMover takes a minimal-data approach by design:

- No conversation content is stored on ContextMover servers
- No conversation content is transmitted to ContextMover servers during capture or migration
- Credential patterns (API keys, Bearer tokens) are automatically redacted from captured content before storage
- Payloads over 500KB are dropped to prevent memory exhaustion
- All Supabase credentials (for users who enable cloud sync) are encrypted client-side before storage — ContextMover servers never see them in plaintext

Single purpose description

The activeTab permission is required to identify which AI platform the user is currently viewing so the extension can activate the correct session capture configuration for that platform. Each supported platform (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Perplexity) has a distinct API response format. When the user opens the side panel or triggers a capture action, the extension reads the active tab's URL to determine which parser to apply. This permission is also used when the user initiates a migration: the extension checks the active tab to confirm the destination platform before injecting the migrated context. activeTab is used only when the user directly interacts with the extension (clicking the icon or using the side panel). scripting justification
Required to inject the fetch interceptor into supported AI platform pages. The interceptor must run in the page's MAIN world context because overriding window.fetch — the only reliable method to capture streaming SSE responses — requires running in the same JavaScript context as the page. DOM scraping cannot capture streaming content reliably. The injected script only reads response bodies already rendered to the user's screen. It does not modify page content, block requests, or alter response data. The original fetch is always called and returned unmodified. All injected code is statically bundled in the extension package. A lightweight toggle button is also injected on all pages via content_scripts to allow users to open the side panel from any website. It only renders a UI button and does not read page content. No remotely sourced code is ever injected or executed.

storage justification
Stores captured AI sessions, preferences, sync state, and usage counters locally. unlimitedStorage justification
ContextMover stores AI conversation sessions locally on the user's device using IndexedDB. Each captured session includes message text, semantic embeddings (vector representations for context search), and structured metadata. A single active developer may accumulate hundreds of sessions across six supported AI platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek), with each session containing dozens of messages. The semantic embedding model (MiniLM-L6-v2) generates 384-dimensional float vectors per text chunk, which are stored locally for instant offline retrieval. Without unlimitedStorage, the default 10 MB quota would be exhausted within days of normal use, causing data loss and degraded functionality. All data remains on-device; no conversation content is transmitted to external servers. The extension never stores data beyond what the user explicitly captures through their own AI interactions.

offscreen justification
Required to run the Attention Engine — a transformer-based ML model compiled to WebAssembly — in a separate offscreen document. The model generates semantic embeddings of captured messages to score relevance, compress long conversations before migration, and enable semantic search. Running this in an offscreen document is essential: the model is CPU-intensive and causes UI freezes when run on the main thread (observed on hardware processing 900+ chunks). The offscreen document yields between processing batches using explicit delays, respects a priority queue that pauses background indexing when the user triggers a migration, and operates entirely locally. No conversation content is sent to external servers. All ML processing happens on the user's device via WebAssembly.

identity justification
ContextMover uses the chrome.identity API exclusively for Google OAuth2 authentication. This serves two purposes: (1) User sign-in via Google to access their ContextMover account for subscription management and usage tracking, and (2) Google Drive AppData scope access to enable optional cross-device sync of conversation sessions. The extension requests only the drive.appdata scope, which limits access to a hidden application-specific folder that cannot read or modify any other files in the user's Google Drive. Authentication tokens are managed securely through Chrome's built-in identity system and are never exposed to web pages or third-party services. Users are prompted with Google's standard consent screen before any access is granted. No identity information is shared with third parties.

alarms justification
ContextMover uses the chrome.alarms API for two periodic background maintenance tasks: (1) Automatic refresh of remote platform configuration data (selector updates) once per hour to ensure the extension adapts to AI platform UI changes without requiring a store update, and (2) Periodic session data synchronization with Google Drive AppData for users who have opted into cross-device sync. These alarms run at low frequency (hourly intervals) to minimize resource usage and battery impact. The alarms API is used instead of setInterval because Chrome MV3 service workers are ephemeral and may be terminated between events. Alarms persist across service worker restarts, ensuring reliable scheduling. No alarms are used for tracking, analytics, or advertising purposes.
