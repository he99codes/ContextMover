Transfer AI chats and conversations between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and other AI platforms. ContextMover lets you move your AI conversation context between assistants and continue exactly where you left off. 

Switch from ChatGPT to Claude without starting over. Move a Gemini conversation to ChatGPT. Transfer long AI chats and preserve important context without manually copying and pasting messages.

---

### How It Works

ContextMover automatically captures the conversation context directly in your browser. It intercepts the API responses that AI platforms send to render on your screen, storing them locally in your browser's IndexedDB so they are ready to transfer at any time.

When you migrate, ContextMover formats the full conversation specifically for the destination model to maximize comprehension. The receiving model picks up naturally where the previous one left off.

### The Attention Engine

Long conversations can contain hundreds of messages that exceed a model's context window. ContextMover includes a local Attention Engine — a transformer-based semantic indexing system that runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. It identifies the most relevant messages for your current task and compresses context intelligently before migration. No conversation data ever leaves your device for this analysis.

### Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Your conversations are yours. All capture happens locally. The Attention Engine runs locally. Sessions are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. When you migrate, context travels directly from your browser to the destination platform — it never passes through ContextMover servers. Our servers only handle account authentication, usage counters, and subscription status.

### Features

• One-Click Chat Transfer — Select a session, choose a destination, click Migrate. Context is injected automatically.
• Automatic Session Capture — Every conversation is captured as you chat. No manual steps or copying required.
• Smart Context Compression — The Attention Engine compresses long sessions to fit destination context windows using semantic similarity scoring.
• Unified Session Library — Browse, search, and revisit all your AI conversations in one local library.
• Local Semantic Search — Search across all captured sessions using natural language. Finds relevant messages across all platforms ranked by semantic relevance. Runs entirely locally.
• Knowledge Synthesizer — Pull specific context chunks from multiple sessions and combine them into a single custom migration.
• Google Drive Backup — Optional bidirectional sync across devices using Google Drive's private appdata folder. Fully optional — ContextMover works completely offline without it.
• Export Sessions — Export any session as XML, Markdown, or plain text for use in your own tools.

### Three Migration Tiers

• Tier 1 — Full Context: The entire conversation is packaged and injected into the destination. Best for short sessions.
• Tier 2 — Smart Summary: An database-driven summary extracts goals, key decisions, and the recent thread. Best for medium-length sessions.
• Tier 3 — Attention Engine: The local model scores every segment for relevance, selects the highest-value chunks, and compresses everything to fit the destination's context window. Best for long sessions.
